# Operate Phase Procedure

**Procedure ID:** APC-AP-600 | **Internal reference:** APC-PHASE-006

[← Back to documentation index](../index.md)

---

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of the Operate phase is to maintain, support, monitor, and govern production analytics assets after deployment.

The Operate phase ensures APC-governed assets remain available, reliable, secure, documented, and aligned with business needs while providing a structured mechanism for operational support, issue management, adoption monitoring, and continuous improvement.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all APC-governed production assets including:

- Data Pipelines
- Data Products
- Lakehouses
- Warehouses
- Semantic Models
- Reports
- Dashboards
- KPI Frameworks
- Certified Analytics Assets
- Approved Practitioner Assets

This procedure applies to activities involving:

- Data Engineering Team (DE)
- BI Analytics Team (BI)
- Business Stakeholders
- APC Representatives
- Support Owners
- Analytics Leadership

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

#### Data Engineering Team (DE)

Responsible for:

- Supporting production data assets
- Monitoring data engineering components
- Supporting data quality investigations
- Resolving DE-owned production issues
- Maintaining DE-owned documentation

#### BI Analytics Team (BI)

Responsible for:

- Supporting production reports and dashboards
- Supporting semantic models
- Supporting KPI implementations
- Monitoring analytics asset health
- Maintaining BI-owned documentation

#### Business Stakeholder

Responsible for:

- Reporting issues
- Providing feedback
- Participating in validation activities as needed
- Identifying enhancement opportunities
- Supporting retirement decisions

#### APC Representatives

Responsible for:

- Monitoring governance compliance
- Reviewing standards-related concerns
- Reviewing certification impacts
- Escalating unresolved governance issues

#### Analytics Leadership

Responsible for:

- Resolving escalated support issues
- Supporting prioritization decisions
- Reviewing operational performance when required

## 4. Definitions

#### Operational Asset

A production analytics asset currently being used to support business operations or decision-making.

#### Incident

An unplanned interruption, degradation, failure, or data issue affecting an APC-governed asset.

#### Data Quality Issue

A condition where delivered data is inaccurate, incomplete, inconsistent, or does not meet expected business requirements.

#### Operational Support

Activities performed to maintain solution availability, reliability, security, and usability.

#### Enhancement Request

A request for additional functionality, modifications, improvements, or new requirements for an existing asset.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Accept Operational Ownership

Upon completion of the Deploy phase, operational ownership shall be established for the production asset.

Ownership shall identify:

- BI responsibilities
- DE responsibilities
- Business owner
- Applicable support contacts

### 5.2 Monitor Production Assets

Production assets shall be monitored to ensure continued operation and availability.

Monitoring activities may include:

- Data refreshes
- Pipeline execution
- Report availability
- Semantic model availability
- Data quality indicators
- General operational health

### 5.3 Manage Support Requests

Support requests shall be reviewed, prioritized, assigned, and resolved according to approved support processes.

Support activities may include:

- Issue investigation
- Data issue resolution
- Report issue resolution
- Access-related support
- User assistance

### 5.4 Manage Operational Issues

Operational issues impacting APC-governed assets shall be investigated and resolved.

Issues should be documented and tracked according to approved support processes.

Where applicable, root causes should be identified and corrective actions implemented.

### 5.5 Monitor Data Quality

Data quality concerns identified by business users, BI, DE, or APC stakeholders shall be reviewed and addressed.

Data quality activities may include:

- Issue investigation
- Data validation
- Root cause analysis
- Corrective actions

### 5.6 Maintain Documentation

Documentation shall be reviewed and updated as changes occur.

Documentation may include:

- Technical documentation
- Business documentation
- Operational support documentation
- KPI documentation
- Metadata records

### 5.7 Review Usage and Adoption

Usage and adoption information should be reviewed to understand how APC-governed assets are being utilized.

Information may be used to support:

- Enhancement decisions
- Retirement decisions
- Continuous improvement activities
- APC reporting activities

### 5.8 Identify Enhancement Opportunities

Requests for changes or improvements shall be evaluated and routed through the Enhance phase as appropriate.

### 5.9 Identify Retirement Candidates

Assets that are no longer utilized, no longer supported, duplicated, replaced, or no longer aligned with business needs should be evaluated for retirement.

Approved retirement candidates shall transition to the Retire phase.

## 6. Inputs

The following inputs may initiate the Operate phase:

- Completed Deployment Activities
- Production Validation Results
- Operational Ownership Assignment
- Hypercare Completion
- Approved Documentation

## 7. Outputs

The Operate phase may produce:

- Support Records
- Incident Records
- Operational Issue Logs
- Data Quality Investigations
- Adoption Information
- Documentation Updates
- Enhancement Recommendations
- Retirement Recommendations

The following unresolved APC decisions may impact execution of this phase.

#### APC-OD-005: Usage & Adoption Metrics

Standardized definitions, reporting requirements, and ownership for adoption and usage metrics remain under review.

Requirements for maintaining KPI definitions, glossary terms, lineage records, metadata, and report documentation remain under review.
