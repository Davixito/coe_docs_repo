# Discovery / Define Phase Procedure

**Procedure ID:** APC-AP-100 | **Internal reference:** APC-PHASE-001

[← Back to documentation index](../index.md)

---

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of the Discovery / Define phase is to evaluate, classify, prioritize, and define analytics initiatives to ensure work aligns with business objectives, APC governance requirements, enterprise standards, architectural principles, and organizational priorities.

This phase establishes a common understanding of the business need, desired outcomes, scope, stakeholders, success criteria, and expected value before resources are committed to detailed design and development activities.

The Discovery / Define phase serves as the primary intake and decision-making point within the APC Project Lifecycle.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all APC-governed analytics initiatives, including but not limited to:

- New reporting requests
- New semantic model requests
- New data product requests
- New KPI requests
- Data integration initiatives
- Enterprise analytics initiatives
- Enhancement requests requiring formal evaluation
- Practitioner certification and promotion requests
- Strategic analytics initiatives

This procedure applies to activities involving:

- Business Stakeholders
- BI Analytics Team (BI)
- Data Engineering Team (DE)
- Analytics Leadership
- Practitioners

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

#### Business Stakeholder

Responsible for:

- Identifying business need
- Defining expected outcomes
- Providing business requirements
- Participating in discovery discussions
- Identifying business validation resources

#### BI Analytics Team (BI)

Responsible for:

- Evaluating reporting requirements
- Assessing analytics impacts
- Assessing KPI requirements
- Evaluating semantic model implications
- Estimating BI effort and complexity

#### Data Engineering Team (DE)

Responsible for:

- Evaluating source system availability
- Assessing architecture impacts
- Assessing data requirements
- Assessing integration requirements
- Estimating DE effort and complexity

#### Analytics Leadership

Responsible for:

- Resolving prioritization conflicts
- Supporting strategic alignment
- Approving resource allocation when required
- Resolving escalated issues

#### Practitioner

Responsible for:

- Providing information related to practitioner-developed assets
- Participating in certification or promotion discussions when applicable

## 4. Definitions

#### APC

Advanced Practices Council responsible for analytics governance, standards, enablement, and oversight.

#### Request

Project intake for a new analytics capability, enhancement, certification, support effort requiring project-level work, or strategic initiative.

#### Discovery

The process of gathering information necessary to understand a business need, identify stakeholders, determine expected outcomes, and assess feasibility.

#### Define

The process of documenting scope, requirements, assumptions, constraints, dependencies, and success criteria.

#### Enhancement

A modification or improvement to an existing analytics asset.

#### Practitioner Asset

A report, dataset, semantic model, or other analytics asset developed outside APC ownership.

#### Project Charter

A document establishing initiative purpose, objectives, stakeholders, scope, assumptions, constraints, and success measures.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Request Intake

Analytics work shall enter the APC lifecycle through an approved intake mechanism.

The purpose of intake is to ensure requests are documented, assigned ownership, and evaluated consistently.

Examples include:

- New analytics requests
- New reporting requests
- Data requests
- Enhancement requests
- Practitioner promotion requests
- Strategic initiatives

### 5.2 Request Classification

Requests shall be reviewed and classified to determine the appropriate handling path.

Classification should identify:

- Request type
- Business impact
- Strategic alignment
- Operational impact
- Expected complexity
- Required participating teams

### 5.3 Discovery Activities

Discovery activities shall be performed to understand the problem being solved and establish a common understanding of desired outcomes.

Discovery activities may include:

- Stakeholder discussions
- Requirement gathering sessions
- Process reviews
- Current-state assessments
- Data source reviews
- Impact assessments
- Risk identification

The depth of discovery should be proportional to request complexity and business impact.

### 5.4 Requirements Definition

Requirements shall be documented sufficiently to support prioritization and detailed design activities.

Requirements should identify:

- Business objectives
- Success criteria
- Expected outcomes
- Functional requirements
- Reporting requirements
- KPI requirements
- Data requirements
- Security requirements
- Compliance requirements

### 5.5 Scope Definition

Scope shall be documented and reviewed with appropriate stakeholders.

Scope definition should identify:

- Included deliverables
- Excluded deliverables
- Assumptions
- Constraints
- Dependencies
- Risks
- Resource considerations

### 5.6 Initial Solution Assessment

An initial assessment shall be performed to determine:

- Feasibility
- Data availability
- Architecture considerations
- Governance impacts
- Certification implications
- Standards implications

The objective is to identify significant concerns before detailed design begins.

### 5.7 Prioritization

Requests shall be evaluated using approved APC prioritization criteria.

Prioritization should consider:

- Business value
- Strategic alignment
- Regulatory requirements
- Resource availability
- Technical complexity
- Organizational impact
- Risk

### 5.8 Approval to Proceed

The Discovery / Define phase concludes when sufficient information exists to determine whether the request should proceed.

The request shall be assigned one of the following outcomes:

- Approved for Design
- Deferred
- Placed in Backlog
- Bundled with Related Work
- Returned for Additional Information
- Rejected

### 5.9 Transition to Design

Requests approved to move forward shall transition to the Design phase.

All approved deliverables, supporting documentation, and decision records shall be made available to the Design team.
